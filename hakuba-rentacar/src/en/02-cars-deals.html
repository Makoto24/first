Vehicles & Rates — Hakuba Rent a Car

Hakuba Rent a Car offers three vehicle classes: kei car, compact car and minivan. Every vehicle is 4WD, with winter tires fitted as standard from December to March. All prices below include tax and basic third-party insurance.

🕒 Last updated: August 2026

📌 Key facts about our rates

  • 24-hour rates (tax included): kei car ¥11,000, compact car ¥13,000, minivan ¥24,000.
  • During the green season (April–November) all classes are 40% off: ¥6,600, ¥7,800 and ¥14,400 respectively.
  • In winter (December–March), 3+ days is 10% off and 7+ days is 20% off.
  • Monthly rates are ¥120,000, ¥150,000 and ¥220,000. Longer rentals are quoted individually.
  • Child seats and ski racks are ¥1,100 each per 24 hours, tax included.
  • Vehicle damage coverage is ¥3,300 per day; damage coverage plus NOC waiver is ¥5,500 per day.
  • Shuttle service costs ¥3,000 one way in the Hakuba / Otari area and ¥6,000 for the Omachi area.
  • Vehicles are supplied with a full tank and must be returned full. Advance reservation is required.

🌿 Green Season Campaign — All classes 40% OFF

During the green season (April – November), all vehicle classes receive a 40% discount off the standard 24-hour rate.

⚠️ Exclusion periods: Golden Week (late April – early May), Obon and summer holidays (mid-August), and other peak periods are excluded. Please confirm at the time of booking.

Fuel policy & things to know

Vehicles are supplied with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank. If the tank is not full on return, fuel charges are calculated using our standard formula and billed to you. See our Terms & Conditions / Booking Policies for details.

  • Late returns may incur additional charges.
  • Petrol stations are limited around Hakuba, so allow enough time to refuel before returning.
  • All vehicles are strictly non-smoking, and pets are not allowed.
  • Unpaid parking fines are charged at ¥25,000.

⚠️ Advance reservation required

Walk-in rentals are not available. Every vehicle must be reserved in advance. Please complete your reservation online or by phone before visiting.

FAQ

Questions about pricing

What is the cheapest vehicle at Hakuba Rent a Car?

The cheapest class at Hakuba Rent a Car is the kei car at ¥11,000 per 24 hours including tax. During the green season (April to November) it drops 40% to ¥6,600.

Is insurance included in the price shown?

Yes. The rate includes unlimited bodily injury coverage, unlimited property damage coverage with no deductible, personal injury coverage up to ¥30 million per person, and roadside assistance up to ¥150,000 per accident. Vehicle repair costs and NOC are not included, so we recommend adding vehicle damage coverage (¥3,300 per day) or damage coverage plus NOC waiver (¥5,500 per day).

Are there discounts for longer rentals?

Yes. In the standard season (December to March), rentals of 3 days or more are 10% off and 7 days or more are 20% off. Monthly rates are ¥120,000 for a kei car, ¥150,000 for a compact car and ¥220,000 for a minivan, tax included. Rentals longer than one month are quoted individually.

Can the green season discount be combined with the long-rental discount?

During the green season (April to November), a flat 40% discount applies regardless of rental length. Because 40% is larger than the long-rental discounts of 10% and 20%, the green season rate is the one applied.

What is NOC (Non-Operation Charge)?

NOC is a compensation charge for the income lost while a vehicle is out of service for repair or cleaning after an accident, theft or damage. It is charged in addition to the actual repair costs. At Hakuba Rent a Car it is ¥55,000 if the vehicle can be returned under its own power and ¥110,000 if towing is required, both including tax. Option C (damage coverage plus NOC waiver, ¥5,500 per day) waives NOC entirely.

Do winter tires cost extra?

No. Hakuba Rent a Car fits winter tires to every vehicle from December to March as standard, at no additional charge.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancelling more than 48 hours before pick-up is free. Within 48 hours the fee is 50% of the base rental fee, and a no-show without notice is charged at 100%.

When and how do I pay?

Payment is taken at pick-up, before the rental starts. We accept cash (Japanese yen only), credit cards (VISA, Mastercard, AMEX, JCB, Diners) and QR payments (PayPay, Alipay, WeChat Pay).
