1. Driver's licence & eligibility

You may rent and drive our vehicles if you hold a driver's licence valid for use in Japan. There is no upper age limit. You must present the original licence on the day of rental — copies and photos are not accepted. One of the following is required.

  1. Japanese driver's licence
  2. International Driving Permit (IDP): must be issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention and say "1949 GENEVA". IDPs issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention are not valid in Japan.
  3. Foreign licence with official translation: required for licences issued in Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Slovenia, Monaco and Taiwan — bring the original licence and an official Japanese translation.

* Visitors to Japan must also present a passport.

4. Usage rules

Vehicles are provided with a full tank of fuel and must be returned full. If the tank is not full on return, fuel charges are calculated using our standard formula and billed to you.

Illegal parking

If parking violations are not settled, a penalty fee of ¥25,000 will be charged.

No smoking, no pets

All vehicles are strictly non-smoking, and pets are not allowed.

Advance reservation

Hakuba Rent a Car operates by advance reservation only. We cannot hand over a vehicle without a booking.

FAQ

Questions about our terms

When do cancellation fees start to apply?

Cancelling more than 48 hours before pick-up is free. Within 48 hours of pick-up the fee is 50% of the base rental fee, and a no-show without notice is charged at 100%.

Does the insurance cover every driver?

No. All Hakuba Rent a Car insurance and coverage applies only to drivers aged 21 and over. If someone under 21 drives the vehicle, the coverage does not apply.

What does the basic rate cover?

The rental fee includes unlimited bodily injury coverage, unlimited property damage coverage with no deductible, personal injury coverage up to ¥30 million per person, and roadside assistance up to ¥150,000 per accident. Vehicle repair costs and NOC are not included, so consider adding Plan B (¥3,300 per day) or Plan C (¥5,500 per day).

How much is NOC, and can it be waived?

NOC (Non-Operation Charge) is ¥55,000 if the vehicle can be returned under its own power and ¥110,000 if towing is required, both including tax. It is charged in addition to the actual repair costs. Selecting Plan C (damage coverage + NOC waiver, ¥5,500 per day) waives NOC entirely.

What happens if I get a parking ticket?

If a parking violation is left unsettled, Hakuba Rent a Car charges a penalty fee of ¥25,000. Please settle any fines yourself.

Is there an upper age limit?

No. There is no upper age limit as long as you hold a driver's licence valid for use in Japan. Insurance coverage, however, applies only to drivers aged 21 and over.

Can I smoke in the car or travel with a pet?

No. All Hakuba Rent a Car vehicles are strictly non-smoking, and pets are not allowed. If smoking or pet transport is detected, a cleaning fee may be charged.

What happens if I return the car late?

Late returns incur extension charges. If you expect to be late, call Hakuba Rent a Car on +81-50-1793-0205 in advance. Unauthorised late returns may incur additional charges.
