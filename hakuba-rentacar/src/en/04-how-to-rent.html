How to Rent

How to Rent, Licence Rules & What to Do in an Emergency

A step-by-step guide to renting with Hakuba Rent a Car, from booking to return. This page also covers the driver's licence rules that apply in Japan and what to do if you have an accident or breakdown.

🕒 Last updated: August 2026

⚠ IMPORTANT: Please read our Terms & Conditions / Booking Policies regarding insurance coverage and cancellation fees before you book.

📌 Key facts about renting

  • Hakuba Rent a Car requires an advance reservation — walk-in rentals are not available.
  • Submit the online booking form; a confirmation email follows within 2 business days.
  • On the day you need your original driver's licence, your passport if you are a visitor to Japan, and a payment method.
  • Payment is taken at pick-up: cash (JPY), credit card or QR payment.
  • Vehicles must be returned with a full tank.
  • You need a Japanese licence, a 1949 Geneva Convention IDP, or an eligible foreign licence plus an official Japanese translation.
  • 1968 Vienna Convention IDPs are not valid in Japan.
  • After any accident you must report it to the police (110) — insurance does not apply without a police report.

Rental flow — four steps

1

Booking request

Submit a request through the booking form on our website. We check availability and send a confirmation email within 2 business days. Replies are sent during business hours (8:00–18:00).

2

Arrival

You have two ways to reach us on the day:

  • Come to the branch: the Hakuba Station Branch (4 minutes' walk from JR Hakuba Station) or the Hakuba Norikura Branch (inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge).
  • Shuttle service: we can collect you from your accommodation or the nearest station within the Hakuba and Otari area, for ¥3,000 one way including tax.
3

Handover

What to bring

  • Driver's licence (original)
  • Passport (required for non-residents)
  • Payment method

We then sign the contract, take payment, check the vehicle for existing damage together, and hand over the keys.

4

Return

Return the vehicle to the branch on time with a full tank. Any additional fees are settled at this point.

Drop-off service: after the return we can drive you on to your hotel, the station or another agreed location. One-way rental between our two branches is also possible.

Driver's licence requirements

To drive in Japan you must hold one of the following. If you forget the document on the day, we cannot rent you a vehicle. Copies and digital images are not accepted.

Option 1

Japanese driver's licence

A valid licence issued in Japan. Please present the original. There is no upper age limit.

Option 2

International Driving Permit

An IDP issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention. Check that the booklet says "1949 GENEVA".

Option 3

Foreign licence + translation

An eligible foreign licence together with an official Japanese translation. No IDP is needed in this case.

[Check before you travel]
  • ❌ IDPs issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention (common in EU countries) are not valid in Japan. The booklet must say "1949 GENEVA".
  • ❌ Digital copies and photos are not accepted — you must bring the original paper booklet.
Eligible countries and regions for "foreign licence + official Japanese translation"
Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Slovenia, Monaco, Taiwan

In case of an accident or breakdown

If you are involved in an accident or breakdown, stay calm and follow these steps. Insurance will not cover damages if you fail to report the accident to the police.

FAQ

Questions about renting

How far in advance should I book?

The Hakuba Rent a Car booking form accepts requests 24 hours a day, and we confirm availability by email within 2 business days. We recommend booking early for the winter season (December to March) and for Golden Week and Obon. We also take bookings for the following season, with early-bird discounts available.

Can I just turn up and rent on the day?

No. Hakuba Rent a Car operates by advance reservation only and cannot hand over a vehicle to walk-in customers, even if a car happens to be free. Please send a booking request beforehand.

What do I need to bring on the day?

Bring your original driver's licence, your passport if you are visiting Japan, and a payment method (cash, credit card or QR payment). Copies and digital images of a licence are not accepted, and without the original we cannot rent you a vehicle.

Is my European International Driving Permit valid?

Only if it was issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention — check that the booklet says "1949 GENEVA". IDPs issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention, which are common in EU countries, are not valid in Japan. If your licence was issued in Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Slovenia, Monaco or Taiwan, you can drive with the original licence plus an official Japanese translation, and no IDP is required.

Is there an age limit?

There is no upper age limit as long as you hold a licence valid for use in Japan. Note, however, that all insurance and coverage applies only to drivers aged 21 and over.

What happens if I do not return the car with a full tank?

Fuel charges are calculated using our standard formula and billed to you. Petrol stations are limited around Hakuba, so allow extra time to refuel before your return.

What should I do if I am going to be late returning?

Call Hakuba Rent a Car on +81-50-1793-0205 as soon as you know. Late returns without notice may incur additional charges.

What should I do if I have an accident?

First, call 119 for an ambulance if anyone is injured. Then call 110 to report the accident to the police and obtain a police report, however minor the damage — insurance does not apply without one. Then contact Hakuba Rent a Car on +81-50-1793-0205 and the Accident Support Center on 0120-256-110. If the vehicle cannot be driven, call the Roadside Assistance Desk on 0120-365-110.

Are the cars non-smoking? Can I bring a pet?

All Hakuba Rent a Car vehicles are strictly non-smoking, and pets are not allowed.

Any questions? Just ask.

The booking form is open 24 hours a day. Replies are sent during business hours (8:00–18:00).
