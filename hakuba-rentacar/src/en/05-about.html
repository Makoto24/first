About Hakuba Rent a Car — Car Rental in Hakuba, Nagano

Hakuba Rent a Car is a local car rental service based in Otari Village, Nagano, operated by Be Village Inc. Whether you are heading for the ski slopes, exploring the Japanese Alps, or planning a day trip to see the famous snow monkeys, we provide reliable, friendly car rental in the Hakuba valley in every season.

Beyond car rentals, Be Village partners with local lodges and hotels throughout Hakuba and Otari, and is developing café, massage and wellness services as well as guided tours — including snow monkey tours and fresh sushi tours to the Sea of Japan. Our mission is to create a "village" where travellers feel safe, relaxed and genuinely connected to Japan's nature and culture.

Why rent a car in Hakuba?

The Hakuba and Otari areas are spread across a wide mountain valley, which makes a rental car the most flexible way to get around. With your own vehicle you can reach ski resorts, onsens, hiking trailheads and viewpoints at your own pace, without depending on infrequent local buses.

⛷ Ski where the snow is best

Happo-one, Iwatake, Hakuba Goryu, Tsugaike Kogen, Hakuba Norikura and Cortina are all within reach. With a car you can pick your resort each morning based on conditions.

♨️ More onsen and dining choice

The Hakuba and Otari area has many hot springs and restaurants scattered across the valley. A car means you go where you want, when you want.

🚗 Day trips from Hakuba

Jigokudani Monkey Park, the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route, Kamikochi, Matsumoto Castle and the Sea of Japan coast all make comfortable day trips.
