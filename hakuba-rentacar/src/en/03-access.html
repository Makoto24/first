Locations & Access — Hakuba Rent a Car

Hakuba Rent a Car operates two branches in the Hakuba valley: the Hakuba Station Branch and the Hakuba Norikura Branch. Choose whichever suits your accommodation and travel plans — one-way rental between the two is also possible.

Last updated: August 2026

Key location facts

  • Hakuba Station Branch: 5949-1 Hokujo, Hakuba-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9301 — 4 minutes' walk from JR Hakuba Station.
  • Hakuba Norikura Branch: 12856-125 Chikuni-otsu, Otari-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9422, inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge.
  • Both branches are open 8:00–18:00 (irregular holidays; hours may vary by season).
  • Phone: +81-50-1793-0205. Email: info@hakubarentacar.com.
  • One-way rental between the two branches is available — tell us when you book.
  • Shuttle service costs ¥3,000 one way in the Hakuba / Otari area and ¥6,000 for the Omachi area, tax included.
  • Every rental requires an advance reservation — walk-ins cannot be served.

FAQ

Questions about our locations

Where is the Hakuba Station Branch and how do I get there?

The Hakuba Rent a Car Hakuba Station Branch is at 5949-1 Hokujo, Hakuba-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9301. The nearest station is Hakuba Station on the JR Oito Line, a 4-minute walk away. The branch is open 8:00–18:00.

Where is the Hakuba Norikura Branch?

The Hakuba Rent a Car Hakuba Norikura Branch is at 12856-125 Chikuni-otsu, Otari-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9422, inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge. It sits right by Hakuba Norikura Onsen Ski Resort and Hakuba Cortina Ski Resort, and is open 8:00–18:00.

Which of the two branches should I use?

If you arrive by train or highway bus, or you are heading for Happo-one, Iwatake or Hakuba Goryu, the Hakuba Station Branch is the more convenient choice. If you are staying near Hakuba Norikura, Cortina or Tsugaike Kogen, or arriving by car, choose the Hakuba Norikura Branch. Both offer the same rates and the same vehicles.

Can I pick up at the Hakuba Station Branch and return to the Hakuba Norikura Branch?

Yes. Hakuba Rent a Car allows one-way rental between its two branches. Please tell us your pick-up branch and return branch when you make your reservation.

Can you collect me from my accommodation?

Yes. Within the Hakuba and Otari area the shuttle costs ¥3,000 one way, including tax, and covers Hakuba Station, Happo Bus Terminal, Tsugaike Kogen, Hakuba Norikura, Cortina and Hakuba Goryu. The Omachi area (Shinano-Omachi Station, Omachi Onsenkyo) is ¥6,000 one way, and Nagano or Matsumoto is quoted individually. Please request it when booking.

Can I collect or return the car outside opening hours?

Both Hakuba Rent a Car branches are open 8:00–18:00. If you need to collect or return outside those hours, please ask when you book — whether we can accommodate it depends on the season and the day's schedule.

Do you have regular closing days?

Hakuba Rent a Car has irregular holidays rather than fixed closing days, and hours can change with the season. Please confirm your intended dates through the booking form or by phone on +81-50-1793-0205.

I have a lot of luggage — is the walk from the station manageable?

The Hakuba Station Branch is a 4-minute walk from JR Hakuba Station, but if you are carrying skis, snowboards or large bags we recommend the shuttle service (¥3,000 one way within the Hakuba and Otari area). Please request it when you book.
