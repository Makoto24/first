Why Choose Hakuba Rent a Car

At Hakuba Rent a Car we pride ourselves on offering more than just vehicles — we provide convenience, flexibility and peace of mind for travellers from around the world. Here is why we stand out.

Last updated: August 2026

Eight reasons travellers choose us

  • Every vehicle is 4WD, giving stable driving on Hakuba's steep mountain roads and winter snow.
  • Winter tires are fitted as standard from December to March, and ski racks and tyre chains are available.
  • Rent for one day up to a full ski season — flexible plans for every travel style.
  • Two branches — the Hakuba Station Branch and the Hakuba Norikura Branch — with one-way rental between them.

  • The Hakuba Norikura Branch has an on-site café and accommodation (Hakuba Cortina Lodge).
  • Prices include tax and basic insurance, and the green season brings 40% off every vehicle class.

Flexible rental options

Whether you need a car for just one day, a weekend trip, a week-long holiday, a month, or the entire ski season, we have you covered. Our flexible plans suit every traveller, from short getaways to long stays in the mountains. Rentals of 3 days or more receive 10% off, and 7 days or more receive 20% off.

4WD for safety and confidence

All of our cars are 4WD, ensuring safe and stable driving on mountain roads and in snowy conditions — perfect for Hakuba's alpine terrain. Some rental companies mix two-wheel-drive cars into their fleet; every vehicle we operate is four-wheel drive.

Winter-ready equipment

From December to March we fit studless winter tyres as standard, at no extra charge. We also provide ski and snowboard carriers (¥1,100 per unit per 24 hours, tax included) and tyre chains, so you can head to the slopes with complete confidence. A ski rack fits up to 4 snowboards or 6 pairs of skis.

Two branches and transfers

Hakuba Rent a Car operates two branches: the Hakuba Station Branch (4 minutes' walk from JR Oito Line, Hakuba Station) and the Hakuba Norikura Branch (inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge, near Hakuba Norikura Ski Resort and the bus stop connecting Nagano Station with Hakuba). Choose the one that suits your itinerary — and one-way rental between the two is also available.

We also offer optional transfers to ski slopes, your accommodation or Hakuba Station for an additional fee, which makes a real difference when you are carrying skis, snowboards or large bags.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is every Hakuba Rent a Car vehicle really 4WD?

Yes. Every vehicle at Hakuba Rent a Car — kei cars, compact cars and minivans alike — is 4WD. We hold no two-wheel-drive cars in the fleet, so you get stable driving on Hakuba's steep mountain roads and winter snow whichever class you choose.

Do winter tyres or tyre chains cost extra?

Studless winter tyres are fitted to every vehicle from December to March at no extra charge, and tyre chains are also provided. Only ski and snowboard carriers carry an optional fee, at ¥1,100 per unit per 24 hours including tax.

Can I rent for a whole season?

Yes. Hakuba Rent a Car rents from a single day up to an entire ski season. Monthly rates are ¥120,000 for a kei car, ¥150,000 for a compact car and ¥220,000 for a minivan, tax included. Rentals longer than one month are quoted individually — please contact us.

Do you have English-speaking staff?

Yes. Hakuba Rent a Car has English-speaking staff on hand. The booking form, contract documents and vehicle briefing are all available in both English and Japanese.

How long does pick-up take?

If you have booked online in advance, pick-up takes about 3 minutes from arrival to departure. On the day, please bring your original driver's licence (plus your passport if you are visiting Japan) and a payment method.

Can I book a car and accommodation together?

Yes. The Hakuba Norikura Branch is inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge, so you can book your rental car and your stay together. There is also a café on site for before or after your journey. Please ask when you make your reservation.

Where are your branches?

Hakuba Rent a Car has two branches. The Hakuba Station Branch is at 5949-1 Hokujo, Hakuba-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9301, four minutes' walk from JR Hakuba Station. The Hakuba Norikura Branch is at 12856-125 Chikuni-otsu, Otari-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9422, inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge. Both are open 8:00–18:00 and one-way rental between them is available.
