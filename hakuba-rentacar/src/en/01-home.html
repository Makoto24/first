FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often before a booking.

Where are the Hakuba Rent a Car branches?

Hakuba Rent a Car has two branches. The Hakuba Station Branch is at 5949-1 Hokujo, Hakuba-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9301, four minutes' walk from JR Hakuba Station. The Hakuba Norikura Branch is at 12856-125 Chikuni-otsu, Otari-mura, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9422, inside Hakuba Cortina Lodge. Both are open 8:00–18:00.

Are all vehicles 4WD?

Yes. Every vehicle at Hakuba Rent a Car is 4WD, and winter tires are fitted as standard from December to March, so you can drive snowy and icy mountain roads with confidence.

What does the green season campaign include?

From April to November, all vehicle classes are 40% off. A kei car costs ¥6,600 per 24 hours, a compact car ¥7,800 and a minivan ¥14,400, all including tax. Golden Week, Obon and other peak periods are excluded.

Can I rent without a reservation?

No. Hakuba Rent a Car requires an advance reservation for every rental, and cannot hand over a vehicle to walk-in customers. The booking form accepts requests 24 hours a day; replies are sent during business hours (8:00–18:00).

Can I pick up at one branch and return to the other?

Yes. Hakuba Rent a Car allows one-way rental between the Hakuba Station Branch and the Hakuba Norikura Branch. Please tell us your pick-up and return branch when you book.

Can you collect me from my accommodation?

Yes. Within the Hakuba and Otari area the shuttle costs ¥3,000 one way (¥6,000 return, tax included), covering Hakuba Station, Happo Bus Terminal, Tsugaike Kogen, Hakuba Norikura, Cortina and Hakuba Goryu. The Omachi area is ¥6,000 one way, and Nagano or Matsumoto is quoted individually. Please request it when booking — availability depends on road and booking conditions on the day.

What payment methods do you accept?

Hakuba Rent a Car accepts cash (Japanese yen only), credit cards (VISA, Mastercard, AMEX, JCB, Diners) and QR payments (PayPay, Alipay, WeChat Pay). Payment is taken at pick-up, before the rental starts.

What licence do I need as an overseas visitor?

You need one of the following: a Japanese driver's licence; an International Driving Permit issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention (it must say "1949 GENEVA"); or a licence issued in Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Slovenia, Monaco or Taiwan together with an official Japanese translation. IDPs issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention are not valid in Japan. You must present the original document — copies and digital images are not accepted.

What are the cancellation fees?

Cancelling more than 48 hours before pick-up is free. Within 48 hours the fee is 50% of the base rental fee, and a no-show without notice is charged at 100%.

Can I rent a child seat or a ski rack?

Yes. Child seats and ski racks are ¥1,100 each per 24 hours, tax included. A ski rack fits up to 4 snowboards or 6 pairs of skis. Stock is limited, so please request them when you book.
